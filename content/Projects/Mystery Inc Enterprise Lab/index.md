### Mystery Inc Enterprise Lab

A segmented enterprise home lab built to develop hands-on experience administering, securing, monitoring, and troubleshooting a small business environment.

The project covers:

- Windows Server, Active Directory, and endpoint administration
- Linux systems and services
- Network segmentation, routing, and firewalling with OPNsense
- PowerShell, scripting, and automation
- Backup, recovery, and infrastructure troubleshooting
- Security monitoring, detection, and incident response

The environment is built and documented incrementally, with each change validated and recorded as part of the portfolio.
